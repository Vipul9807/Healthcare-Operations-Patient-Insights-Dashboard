# Healthcare-Operations-Patient-Insights-Dashboard

## Summary:

 An interactive **Excel-based Healthcare Operations & Patient Insights Dashboard** designed to transform raw hospital data into 
 meaningful business insights. The project analyzes patient admissions, billing, insurance providers, medical conditions, hospital 
 performance, admission trends, patient volumes, and length of stay through interactive KPIs, charts, and slicers, enabling users 
 to explore operational and financial patterns and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem:

 Healthcare organizations need a consolidated view of **patient activity, hospital admissions, billing, insurance performance, 
 and medical conditions** to understand operational and financial patterns. Raw patient-level data makes it difficult to 
 quickly identify admission trends, high-volume hospitals, major billing contributors, and differences across insurance 
 providers and medical conditions. This dashboard addresses the need for an **interactive, centralized view of key 
 healthcare metrics** to support operational monitoring, performance analysis, and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Questions Answered:

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

1. **What is the total billing amount and average billing amount across all patients?**
2. **How does the number of patient admissions vary over time?**
3. **Which hospitals have the highest admission volumes?**
4. **Which hospitals account for the highest total patient-stay days?**
5. **How do admission types and test results vary across patients?**
6. **Which medical conditions generate the highest billing amounts?**
7. **Which medical conditions have the highest patient volume?**
8. **Which insurance providers generate the highest billing amounts?**
9. **Which insurance providers have the highest patient volume?**
10. **How does patient volume vary across different demographic segments?**
11. **What is the average length of stay across patients?**
12. **How do key healthcare metrics change when filtered by admission date, gender, medication, or blood type?**

## Methodology

1. **Data Preparation** – Reviewed and structured the hospital dataset, including patient, admission, billing, insurance, medical condition, medication, and test-result information.
2. **Data Cleaning & Validation** – Checked data consistency, formats, missing values, and relevant fields before analysis.
3. **Data Analysis** – Used Excel functions and PivotTables to calculate key metrics such as patient volume, billing amounts, admissions, and length of stay.
4. **KPI Development** – Created key performance indicators for total billing, total admissions, average billing, and average length of stay.
5. **Trend & Comparative Analysis** – Analyzed monthly admission trends and compared hospitals, insurance providers, and medical conditions.
6. **Interactive Dashboard Development** – Built PivotCharts, slicers, filters, and interactive visualizations to allow users to explore the data dynamically.
7. **Dashboard Design** – Applied a consistent corporate-style color palette, clear visual hierarchy, and concise chart titles to present insights in a management-friendly format.

## Skills Used:

**Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, data validation, data standardization, duplicate checking, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers & Filters, KPI development, dashboard development, data visualization, conditional formatting, dashboard formatting.

**Data Analysis:** Exploratory data analysis (EDA), patient volume analysis, admission trend analysis, billing analysis, comparative analysis, hospital performance analysis, insurance provider analysis, medical condition analysis, pattern identification, insight generation.

**Healthcare & Business Analytics:** Healthcare operations analytics, patient analytics, hospital performance analysis, insurance analytics, billing analysis, operational KPI analysis, trend analysis, data-driven decision support, business reporting.

## Results Obtained

- Analyzed **54,966 patient records** covering hospital admissions, billing, insurance, medical conditions, and patient outcomes.
- Identified a **total billing amount of $1.40 BN**, with an average billing amount of approximately **$25.55K per patient**.
- Calculated an **average patient length of stay of 15.5 days**.
- Analyzed admission patterns across **Emergency, Urgent, and Elective** admissions and compared corresponding test results.
- Identified the hospitals with the highest **admission volumes and total patient-stay days**.
- Compared **billing and patient volumes across insurance providers**, with Cigna recording the highest total billing in the dataset.
- Identified **Diabetes** as the medical condition with the highest total billing amount, while **Arthritis** had the highest patient volume.
- Developed interactive visualizations and slicers to allow users to explore healthcare metrics by **admission date, gender, blood type, and medication**.
- Consolidated key operational and financial metrics into an interactive **Excel healthcare analytics dashboard** for easier analysis and reporting.

## Business Recommendations

- **Monitor high-billing medical conditions:** Analyze treatment patterns and resource utilization for conditions contributing significantly to overall billing, with a focus on identifying cost drivers and opportunities for efficient care delivery.

- **Optimize hospital capacity:** Use admission volumes and patient-stay days to identify hospitals experiencing higher patient loads and support better allocation of beds, staff, and other resources.

- **Review insurance-provider performance:** Compare billing amounts and patient volumes across insurance providers to identify significant differences and support more effective operational and financial planning.

- **Track admission trends:** Monitor monthly admission patterns to anticipate changes in patient demand and improve workforce and capacity planning.

- **Analyze length of stay:** Investigate factors contributing to longer patient stays and identify opportunities to improve discharge planning and operational efficiency.

- **Strengthen patient-segment analysis:** Use demographic and patient attributes available in the dashboard to identify meaningful variations in patient volumes, admissions, and billing.

- **Establish regular KPI monitoring:** Use the dashboard as a recurring reporting tool to track billing, admissions, patient volume, and length-of-stay metrics and support data-driven operational decisions.
